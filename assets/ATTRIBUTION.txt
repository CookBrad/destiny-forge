Rebuild third-party source packs
--------------------------------

  python3 tools/download_assets.py

Downloads itch.io packs into assets/source/ and extracts to:
  source/pixel_crawler/
  source/0x72/
  source/slimes/

Curate gameplay files from source into player/, dungeon/enemies/, and dungeon/environment/.
