Hunter (dungeon player)
  Original grit-neon hunter (Taste-signed idle, run lean, attack chamber, and attack thrust, 2026-08-28).
  Gameplay filenames stay knight_* for loaders.
  Cell 343×160 1× nearest-neighbor. Sword is baked into the hunter.
  Signed cells stay. Added frames are in-house (tools/draw_hunter_frames.py) and add no third-party credit.

Rebuild third-party source packs
--------------------------------

  python3 tools/download_assets.py

Downloads itch.io packs into assets/source/ and extracts to:
  source/pixel_crawler/
  source/0x72/
  source/slimes/

Curate gameplay files from source into player/, dungeon/enemies/, and dungeon/environment/.
