All sprites in assets/sprites/ are AI-generated art processed by tools in tools/.

Raw sources (assets/source/ai_*_raw.jpg):
  player              - tools/character_art.py
  mine_entrance       - tools/mine_art.py
  hub_tiles           - tools/asset_art.py
  dungeon_sheet       - tools/asset_art.py
  forge               - tools/asset_art.py
  hub_background      - tools/asset_art.py
  dungeon_background  - tools/asset_art.py

Rebuild everything:
  .venv/bin/python tools/generate_ai_assets.py

Rebuild gameplay sprites only:
  .venv/bin/python tools/generate_sprites.py
